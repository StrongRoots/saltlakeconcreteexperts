Concrete Work West Jordan Homeowners Ask For

West Jordan is one of the fastest-growing cities in the Salt Lake Valley, and that shows in the concrete work: new driveways and RV pads for growing families, patio additions as people settle into new builds, and driveway replacements in the older neighborhoods east of Redwood Road where 20- and 30-year-old concrete is giving out to freeze-thaw cycles.

  • New driveways — poured to proper thickness with reinforcement, sloped away from the garage
  • RV & trailer pads — thickened concrete that handles real weight without cracking
  • Patios — plain, stamped, or exposed aggregate for backyard living space
  • Sidewalks & walkways — front walks, side yards, and paths to sheds and gates
  • Driveway replacement — tear-out and re-pour of heaved, cracked, or spalling concrete

A note on West Jordan soil and winters

Much of West Jordan sits on clay-heavy soil that expands and contracts with moisture — exactly the kind of ground that punishes a poorly prepped base. Ask any contractor you talk to how they're compacting the sub-base and what thickness and reinforcement they're pouring. The ones who answer confidently are the ones whose driveways survive a decade of Utah winters.

West Jordan Concrete FAQs

Do I need a permit for a concrete driveway in West Jordan?

West Jordan City typically requires a permit for driveway approaches that tie into the street, and inspections apply. Reputable contractors pull the permits as part of the job — confirm it's included in your quote rather than assumed.

How thick should a residential driveway be in West Jordan?

Four inches is the common minimum for passenger vehicles; five to six inches with reinforcement is smarter if you'll park trucks, trailers, or an RV on it. Given West Jordan's clay soils, proper base compaction matters at least as much as thickness.

Can I get an RV pad poured next to my driveway?

Yes — RV pads are one of the most requested add-ons in West Jordan. They need thicker concrete (usually 6 inches) and a well-compacted base to carry the weight. Check your HOA and city setback rules before you plan the location.

How long does a driveway replacement take?

Most residential tear-outs and re-pours take 2–4 days of active work, plus curing time — plan on about a week before you can drive on it. Your contractor will give you an exact schedule with your quote.

Is stamped concrete a good choice for a West Jordan patio?

Yes, it's popular for patios here — the look of stone or pavers at a lower installed cost. Keep it sealed every couple of years to protect the color and surface from freeze-thaw and de-icers.
